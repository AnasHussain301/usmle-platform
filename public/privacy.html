Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. This document outlines how Synapse collects, uses, and safeguards information when you study on synapses.systems.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy Cookie Policy Editorial & Review Policy Contact Us

1 Information We Collect

We collect minimal, necessary data required to personalize your USMLE study sessions and maintain platform security:

  • Google Account Information: When you authenticate via Google OAuth, we receive your Google user ID, name, email address, and profile picture avatar. We do not store or have access to your Google password.
  • Study & Progress Data: We store your last read First Aid book page, Mehlman HY rating marks, flashcard recall intervals, question answers, test scores, and personal study notes.
  • Security & Concurrency Telemetry: To enforce single-user fair-use policies and protect medical content from unauthorized scraping, our server logs IP addresses, browser user agent strings, and active session timestamps.

3 Your Rights Under GDPR (Europe) and CPRA (California)

Regardless of your location, we honor standard global privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request an export of all study data linked to your Google account.
  • Right of Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated study telemetry by contacting us.
