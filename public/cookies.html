Cookie Policy

⚙️ Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You can change your consent settings at any time. Under GDPR and CPRA, you have the right to decline non-essential cookies.

1 What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you access websites. Similar browser storage technologies like localStorage and sessionStorage allow our web application to store essential session state, user interface preferences (such as your chosen font and dark theme), and offline flashcard progress directly in your browser.

2 Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize the cookies and storage keys used on Synapse into three distinct groups:

A. Strictly Necessary (Essential) Cookies

Required

These cookies and local storage items are essential for you to navigate the site, use authentication, and access secure content. They cannot be turned off.

Key / Cookie Name Provider Purpose Duration
synapse_token Synapse JWT token verifying your authenticated Google login session 30 Days
synapse_user Synapse Caches user profile (name, email, role, 30-day trial remaining) Persistent
synapse_cookie_consent Synapse Remembers your GDPR/CPRA cookie consent preference 1 Year
synapse_theme, synapse_font Synapse Stores chosen visual appearance (Dark/Light/Black mode, font family) Persistent

B. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Optional (Consent-Based)

We partner with Google AdSense to display educational and relevant advertising. Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to Synapse or other websites.

Cookie Name Provider Purpose Duration
__gads, __gpi Google AdSense Measures ad impressions, prevents fraudulent clicks, limits repetition 13 Months
IDE, DSID Google DoubleClick Used by Google to serve targeted, personalized ads across partner sites 1 Year

C. Performance & Study Analytics

Optional

These cookies allow us to monitor question answering latency, book scrubber performance, and error frequencies to keep our medical platform fast and reliable.

3 How to Control and Opt Out of Cookies

You have multiple ways to manage or reject tracking cookies:

  • Our Cookie Banner: Click at any time to disable advertising cookies.
  • Google Ad Settings: You can opt out of personalized advertising across all Google services by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry Opt-Out Portals: You can opt out of personalized advertising from participating network advertisers at www.aboutads.info/choices or Your Online Choices (Europe).
  • Browser Controls: All major modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) allow you to block third-party cookies or clear your cache in Settings > Privacy and Security.
