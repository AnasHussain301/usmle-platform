Our Editorial Commitment

"Content reviewed by medical students and MD clinicians according to the latest USMLE guidelines." Every high-yield concept, differential diagnosis matrix, pharmacology mnemonic, and board-style question vignette on Synapse is created and cross-checked against standard medical references to ensure students prepare with genuine, authoritative knowledge.

1 Content Development & Medical Review Process

Synapse produces educational materials designed to optimize active recall, spaced repetition, and deep clinical understanding for medical licensing exams. Our content development pipeline follows a strict four-stage verification protocol:

1 Primary Drafting

Authored by senior medical students and medical graduates who have successfully passed USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK with high percentiles.

2 Clinical Peer Review

Each clinical card, pearl, and explanation is evaluated for diagnostic validity, mechanistic accuracy, and absence of outdated terminology.

3 USMLE Guideline Alignment

Cross-referenced directly with the official USMLE Content Outline (FSMB/NBME) and major board review curricula (First Aid, Pathoma, Sketchy).

4 Continuous Errata Monitoring

Community feedback and student bug reports are analyzed by our editorial team with updates deployed in scheduled review cycles.

2 Core Reference Standards & Textbooks

Our team synthesizes medical concepts based exclusively on established, authoritative medical literature, including:

  • USMLE Composite Examination Guidelines: Federation of State Medical Boards (FSMB) and National Board of Medical Examiners (NBME).
  • Internal Medicine & Pathology: Robbins & Cotran Pathologic Basis of Disease (10th ed.) and Harrison’s Principles of Internal Medicine.
  • Medical Physiology: Costanzo Physiology (7th ed.) and Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology.
  • Pharmacology & Mechanism of Action: Katzung’s Basic & Clinical Pharmacology (15th ed.).
  • Microbiology & Immunology: Levinson Review of Medical Microbiology & Immunology and Janeway’s Immunobiology.
  • Board Synthesis: First Aid for the USMLE Step 1 (McGraw-Hill).

3 Errata Reporting & Correction Protocol

We believe absolute transparency is the cornerstone of trustworthy medical education. If you discover a typographical error, ambiguous question option, or outdated pharmacological guideline anywhere on our platform:

  1. Submit the Report: Use our contact form with the topic, page number, or question ID, or message our WhatsApp helpline at +92-3238057305.
  2. Editorial Investigation: Our medical review board verifies the cited literature within 48 business hours.
  3. Live Patch Deployment: Verified corrections are updated immediately in our central database, updating all web reader and flashcard sessions.

4 Editorial Independence & Commercial Transparency

Synapse maintains complete editorial independence. Our study materials and clinical questions are never influenced, sponsored, or directed by pharmaceutical companies, medical device manufacturers, or hospital networks. Advertising served through Google AdSense is strictly contextual and segregated from editorial content.
